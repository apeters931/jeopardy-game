# Jeopardy!

**[Play the game](https://alecpeters610.pythonanywhere.com/)**

Play a classic Jeopardy! episode in your browser, with the original air date shown
in the title. Choose a category and clue value, type your response, and build your
score through Jeopardy!, Double Jeopardy!, and Final Jeopardy! Daily Doubles and
Final Jeopardy! let you wager your points.

A new episode is published by the daily data build. Refresh the page or choose
**Check for a new game** after finishing to pick up the latest published board.
Your progress is saved in your browser so you can come back to an unfinished game.

Answers allow small spelling mistakes as well as differences in capitalization,
punctuation, accents, and standard “What is…” phrasing. If an equivalent answer
is marked wrong, choose **Count my answer as correct** on the answer screen to
correct your score.

For local setup, hosting, and development, see the [game guide](game/README.md).

This is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Jeopardy!.
